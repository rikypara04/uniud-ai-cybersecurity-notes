# MSc Artificial Intelligence & Cybersecurity

Personal study repository for the Master's Degree in Artificial
Intelligence & Cybersecurity at the University of Udine.

## Courses

- Complexity and Information Theory
- Automated Reasoning
- Foundations of Cybersecurity and Ethics

## Repository structure

- `00-shared`: styles and resources genuinely shared across courses
- `01-complexity-information-theory`: notes and source material for the course
- `02-automated-reasoning`: notes and source material for the course
- `03-foundations-cybersecurity-ethics`: notes and source material for the course

## General principle

Each course is organised independently according to:

- the official material provided by the lecturers;
- the structure of the handwritten Goodnotes notes;
- the practical activities required by the course;
- the relevant academic references.

No common internal structure is imposed on all courses in advance.
